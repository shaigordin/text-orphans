The ten reference traditions are defined from the literature: the Hurrian groups of Gordin, Yavasan & Romach 2025 (StBoT 73, Table 3), the Luwian, Ḫuwaššanna and Palaic festivals as the HFR edition modules divide them, Kizzuwatna rituals (Miller 2004 and others), the Luwian rituals of Puriyanni and Kuwattalla (Yakubovich & Mouton 2023), Arzawa / western rituals (Bawanypeck 2005, Mouton 2010) and the Hattian cult layer (Klinger 1996). A fragment is placed only when its nearest tradition beats the runner-up by at least a margin calibrated on held-out windows to about 80% precision. A further check flags a fragment that fits none of them: either its best similarity is below what 95% of reference fragments of its size reach, or it sits nearest to a genre outside the reference (myths, prayers, oracles, treaties, instructions, cult inventories, other festivals).
