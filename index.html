Orphans & Archetypes

Finding a home for every Hittite fragment

Where does a Hittite fragment belong? This tool compares any fragment with the entire digitised Hittite corpus, word by word and sign by sign, and shows the textual tradition, the compositions and the manuscripts it is closest to, with the evidence for each.
