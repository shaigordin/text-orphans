Adoptio

A quantitative tool for placing Hittite fragments

Where does a Hittite fragment belong? The tool compares it with every manuscript of the digitised Hittite corpus (TLHdig), by its words and their spellings, and names the tradition, compositions and manuscripts it is closest to, with the words each result rests on.
